Why this exists

Caring for a parent in Jamaica from Atlanta, London or Toronto shouldn't mean guessing.

Thousands of Jamaicans living overseas are supporting parents and relatives back home — arranging care, paying for repairs, worrying about falls and hurricanes from a distance. Caribbean Connection gathers what you need in one place: trusted regional news, agencies and programs, upcoming events, and GID's own guidance on healthy, accessible homes adapted for Caribbean living.

Launching with Jamaica as our foundation. We'll add other Caribbean countries as we develop reliable sources and relationships on the ground.
