Meet the founder
“I believe the best spaces are thoughtfully designed around the people who use them.”

Grace Williams is a Registered Interior Designer and Principal of Grace In Design Services, with more than 25 years of experience in interior design, project management and the built environment. Her work spans commercial interiors, workplace, retail, healthcare and residential projects — from space planning and construction documentation to renovations, home assessments and Aging In Place design.

Today she brings that experience together with a growing focus on healthy homes, accessibility, wellness and Aging In Place — helping people create spaces that support how they live now while considering how their needs may change over time.
